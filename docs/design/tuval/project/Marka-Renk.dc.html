Lime is used sparingly.

One lime panel or one lime primary button in view at a time; the top-menu button doesn't count. Beyond that, lime fills only the selected category in the gallery filter. Accent text (lime in dark, dark green in light) marks links, one phrase of the page headline, shot lengths, the free offer, and a job that is running or done.

No lime text in light.

On a light background, lime has a ratio of 1.2. Text and icons take the dark green accent text instead. The button fill stays the same lime in both themes, and its label has a ratio of 14.9.

Status color never speaks alone.

Lime means running or done, orange means stopped, gray means waiting. Each one comes with a label and its own shape.

On video, no themes.

A tag on top of a video is the same in both themes: dark scrim, light text. Even over a pure white frame the ratio is 8.5. The Free tag keeps the scrim and takes lime text.
