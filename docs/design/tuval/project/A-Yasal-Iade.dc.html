Refund policy

Last updated [DATE]

Unused packs

If you have not used any credit from a pack, we refund it in full within 14 days of the purchase. Every request for an unused pack within 14 days is approved.

Used credits

A credit counts as used when a video is delivered or a template is prepared with it. Credits that came back from a stopped job do not count as used. Once a credit from a pack is used, the pack is not refunded. The remaining credits stay valid; credits do not expire.

Videos that are not delivered

If a video is not delivered, its credits go back to your balance on their own. You see it in your account as Credits returned. This is a return of credits, not a refund of money, so there is nothing to ask for.

A video that came out wrong

Tell us within 7 days: open the result and choose Report a problem. We look at it within 2 business days. If the video is wrong, we generate it again at no cost.

How to ask for a refund

Open Account, go to Purchases and choose Request a refund on the pack. While a request is open, the credits of that pack are held. You can cancel the request and use them again. If a refund does not go through, the credits return to your balance. You can also contact support or write to [SUPPORT EMAIL].

After you ask

In Purchases the pack shows where your request is. The example is a Starter pack: 10 credits, $19.

Refund requested
We answer within 2 business days. The 10 credits are held until then.
Refund sent
10 credits were removed. The money is on its way to your payment method; this takes a few days.
Refunded
Refunded on [DATE]. $19 went back to your payment method.
Refund failed
