Voice

Viral Clone says little and says it clearly: the outcome first, then the number. Every sentence in the left column comes from the screens.

Outcome first.

A sentence opens with what happened, then gives the reason.

Numbers, not adjectives.

"About 7 min" instead of "fast", "1080p" instead of "high quality".

Short and direct.

Speak to "you", one action at a time: "Upload your products."

Errors give direction.

What happened, what was saved, what to do now. No apologies, no exclamation marks.

Facts, not promises.

Never "this will go viral". Say "reference 1.9M views".

Glossary: one name per thing

Clone it
The name of the main action. Written the same way in the gallery and on the template page.
template
The shot-by-shot structure of a video. Never preset, theme, or format.
shot, clip
A shot is one piece of the template. A clip is the video generated for that shot. The list is called "Shot list". Never scene.
sample, reference
A sample is our own output playing in the gallery. The reference is the original video the structure came from; it is never shown in the gallery.
generate
The one verb. Never create, make, or render.
credit
The unit you spend. Never token, point, or coin.
returned, refund
Credits are returned to your balance. Money is refunded to your payment method. Never one word for the other.

Writing rules

  • Sentence case only. Headlines take a period, buttons don't.
  • A space between number and unit, a point for decimals: 8 sec, 7 min, 1.9M views.
  • An unknown value goes in square brackets: [BALANCE].
  • No exclamation marks or emoji. An ellipsis only while something is running: "Uploading… 60%".
  • The brand name is Viral Clone in a sentence and viral clone in the logo. It is never translated and never written as one word.
  • One tagline per screen height. A long page may open with the promise and close with the call.
