Components: messages and money

Everything that says what happened and what it costs: inline messages, field errors, the could-not-load block, upload states, credit rows, and the two words for giving something back.

Inline message

Preparing a template costs 1 credit. You confirm before anything is charged.

info: something to know before you act

This video is longer than 60 seconds. Trim it and try again. Nothing was charged.

warning: something stopped or changed; the first sentence says what

Sent again. You can send another in 60 seconds.

success: something finished
Saved. a one-word result next to its button: a status line, no box

A message sits where the thing happened, not at the top of the page. All three share one box; the icon and the edge tell them apart, so the kind reads without color. One or two sentences: what happened, then what to do. A warning that follows a payment or a credit step ends with what was charged.

Field error

The field keeps what was typed. Its edge takes the warning color and one line under it says what to do. The line carries an icon, so it reads without color. Other fields are not touched.

Could not load

Could not load the gallery.

Check your connection and try again. Your videos and credits are not affected.

Contact support after a second failure

When a list or a part of a page cannot load, this block takes its place: an icon, a 20 px title that names what did not load, one 15 px sentence, and a bordered Try again. The button is never lime. After a second failure, Contact support appears beside it.

A thumbnail is 72 by 96 with an 8 px corner. While it uploads, the image is dimmed and a bar on a scrim shows the progress; the percent is written beside it. A failed upload takes a warning ring and icon. An error touches only its own file: the other images and fields stay. The main button stays off until every upload is done.

You are charged when you confirm. If the video is not delivered, the credits come back.

Wherever credits are about to be charged, three rows in this order: the price, the balance now, the balance after. The price row is one step larger and its number takes the accent text color. The price is fixed: the number shown is the number charged. Only time is an estimate ("about 7 min"). A note under the rows says when the charge happens and what comes back.

Returned and refund

returned

Credits go back to your balance. It happens on its own.

  • "Credits returned"
  • "Your credit was returned."
  • "The 2 credits for this video are back in your balance."

refund

Money goes back to your payment method, through the payment provider.

  • "Request a refund"
  • "Refunded"
  • "The money is on its way to your payment method; this takes a few days."

Two words for two different things, never one for the other. A stopped job or a template that could not be prepared returns credits. A pack that was not used is refunded.

A talking template is not covered by the free video. Where a no-talking template says Free with your first video, its page says what it costs: the same line, with the credits icon and no accent color. In the form, a note under the product description says where the presenter's words come from. The sample pages on the canvas show a no-talking template, so these two lines are recorded here.
